<objective>
Make the Exhibit Detail discrepancy actions safe and singular (T-05 from the external UI/UX review): convert the discrepancy banner's plain-text `<button>` triggers (Record ruling / Acknowledge) into real Carbon `Button` components matching the app's other primary buttons, and remove the duplicate "Record ruling" button currently rendered on the right-rail `ObjectionCard` — leaving exactly ONE Record-ruling entry point (the banner's primary button) and the Objection card as details-only.

Purpose: a tiny underlined text link for a judicial-ruling action is an accidental-click risk, and a second independent Record-ruling control targeting the same objectionId is a genuine duplicate-entry-point defect, not just a style inconsistency.
Output: one Carbon-styled banner with one Record-ruling entry point; an Objection card that shows grounds/elapsed-time only.
</objective>
